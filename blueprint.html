Nine products

Dash is one platform made of nine products. Each is grouped by who it serves — retail, logistics, the field, the infrastructure between them, and the platform itself.

01 — Foundation

Vision

A unified logistics platform that connects retailers with logistics providers, powered by a smart routing engine and an open network.

What makes Dash different

  • DMS, Marketplace and Network in one platform
  • The same entity can be supply and demand
  • Freelancers, 3PLs and DMS under one routing engine
  • DMS clients opt into the network on their own terms
  • One centralised Developer Portal for every product
  • No single competitor does all of this together

Where Dash stops

Dash is purely B2B. Its responsibility ends at the merchant — Dash reports pickup, transit and delivery, and the merchant handles everything with their own customer. No end-customer product, no end-customer touchpoint.

02 — Foundation

Segments

Two sides of the market, plus the individuals who move between them.

Retailers — Dash Merchant
Any business that wants to send orders. Restaurants, cafes, groceries, pharmacies, online shops. Access via dashboard or API.
Logistics — Dash DMS
Companies that need to manage their fleet. “Manage your fleet smarter.” Access via dashboard or API.
Logistics — Network Supply
Companies that want more orders. “Get more orders, grow your business.” Through Dash DMS or their own system.
Logistics — Network Demand
Companies with overflow they cannot deliver. “Never say no to a customer again.” Through Dash DMS or their own system.
Freelancers
Individuals looking for flexible delivery work. “Turn your vehicle into income.” Through the Dash Freelancer App.

Where the Dash team runs the platform: verification, client oversight, network and marketplace control, billing, support and platform-wide analytics.

Administrative only. Dash's own fleet is treated as an ordinary DMS client with its own account. Admin runs the platform, not Dash's delivery operation.
01

Authentication and Access

  • Internal team login
  • Two factor authentication
  • Session management
02

Internal Team Management

  • Add and manage Dash team members
  • Roles and permissions
    • Super Admin
    • Operations
    • Finance
    • Support
    • Custom roles
03

Platform Dashboard

  • Total orders across the platform
  • Active orders and active supply
  • Volume by product and by source
  • Merchant distribution by integration type
  • Volume by source channel
  • Integration health across the platform
  • Growth and health indicators
04

Client Management

  • Centralised profiles for all entities
  • Merchants, DMS clients, 3PLs, freelancers, drivers
  • Per client
    • Full profile and account details
    • Subscription and plan
    • Order volume and performance
    • Network and marketplace participation
    • Integration method — connector, API or manual
    • Connection health
    • Suspend or reactivate account
05

Client Verification

  • Verification queue — Merchant, DMS, 3PL
  • Review business documents
  • Verify or reject with reason
  • Verification status per client
  • Unverified state
    • Can log in, view and configure
    • Cannot create records
    • Cannot join Dash Network
    • Cannot list on or connect via Marketplace
06

Freelancer Management

  • Onboarding request queue
  • Document review — National ID, license, vehicle
  • Approve or reject with reason
  • Activation
  • Document expiry monitoring and alerts
  • Auto suspend or flag on expiry
  • Resubmission and re-approval
07

Global Control Tower

  • Every order across the platform
  • Filter by client, source, status, zone, product
  • Live map, platform wide
  • Order details and full history
  • Alerts for stuck, delayed and unfulfilled orders
  • Intervention scope
    • Direct and Marketplace orders — read only, the owner intervenes
    • Dash Network orders — full control: reassign, cancel, escalate
